Desktop app for browsing anime and manga, keeping a watch list, and picking up where you left off.

## Features

- Home page with a featured title, latest episodes, top airing, and upcoming
- Anime and manga browse
- Manga reader
- Watch animes, sub and dub
- Direct streams preferred, with embed when that is all a server offers
- Resume from saved watch progress
- Continue watching, full history, and the option to remove a title
- Release inbox for anime on your list
- Schedule
- Local profiles you can switch between, with a picture and an 18+ switch
- Settings for your profile, scoring, theme, 18+ titles, AniList import, and sync
- Library (metadata) stored on your PC everything is local
